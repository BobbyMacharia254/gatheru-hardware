Your hardware shop in Kagumo

Gatheru Hardware is on the road between Karatina and Kerugoya. Builders, fundis and households come here for cement, roofing, paint, bathroom fittings and tools, and for blank keys.

The shop is run by Ng'ang'a Robert Kaiguri. Come in, tell us what you are building, and we will help you pick what you need.
